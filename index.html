About

I am a marine geophysicist with expertise in near-surface geophysical methods, gas hydrates, methane systems, and carbon cycling in marine environments. My research integrates multibeam echo sounder (MBES) data, seismic interpretation, geochemical modeling, and reactive transport simulations to understand gas seepage, subseafloor processes, and climate-relevant carbon dynamics.

Research Interests

  • Seafloor gas seepage and acoustic characterization
  • Gas hydrate stability and methane cycling
  • CO₂ sequestration and subsurface carbon storage
  • Reactive transport modeling in marine sediments
  • Near-surface and shallow marine geophysics

Current Focus

My current work focuses on morphological and acoustic characterization of gas seepage features offshore Corpus Christi using multibeam echo sounder data, combined with numerical modeling to assess methane fluxes and environmental impact.
