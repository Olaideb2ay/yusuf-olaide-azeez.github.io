About

I am a geoscientist and a Ph.D. candidate in geophysics from Texas A&M University-Corpus Christi, I specialize in gas hydrate modeling, reservoir characterization, static and dynamic model, and reaction transport modeling (RTM). I have gained practical experience through my involvement in various seismic data acquisition and interpretation, well log analysis and research cruises to the Mediterranean Sea, Dead Sea, Corpus Christi Bay and Ross Sea in Antarctica. With a diverse background in geophysics and marine geoscience, I bring broad knowledge and skills in data acquisition, processing and interpretation in near-surface geophysical methods such as electrical resistivity tomography (ERT), Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR), and other geophysics techniques like gravity and magnetic.

Current Focus

Ongoing research investigates morphological and acoustic characterization of gas seepage systems in the Gulf of Mexico, integrating bathymetric mapping, water-column acoustics, and numerical modeling to quantify methane flux and evaluate environmental impact.
