Research Overview

My research integrates marine geophysics, computational modeling, and geochemical analysis to investigate gas hydrate systems, methane dynamics, and carbon storage in shallow marine environments. I combine field-based geophysical data acquisition with advanced numerical modeling approaches to quantify subsurface processes relevant to energy systems and climate.

Research Themes

  • Near-surface marine geophysics and seafloor processes
  • Gas seepage detection using multibeam echo sounder (MBES)
  • Methane cycling and gas hydrate systems
  • Carbon sequestration and CO₂–CH₄ exchange mechanisms
  • Multiscale reactive transport and numerical simulation

Field & Analytical Methods

  • Multibeam bathymetry and water-column acoustics
  • Sub-bottom profiling and seismic interpretation
  • Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ERT)
  • Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR)
  • FDEM, TDEM, gravity and magnetic methods
  • Reactive transport modeling (RTM)
